# Zone

- Bounded context: swallow-wide. `api-server` and `dashboard` use this term with the same meaning.
- Definition: A swallow-owned, Site-scoped logical grouping of Servers — an availability, fault, or organizational grouping — that swallow defines and manages rather than merely observes. When the Site's provisioner supports an equivalent grouping (a MAAS *physical zone*), swallow realizes the Zone in that provisioner as it is created, renamed, deleted, or assigned; when the provisioner has no such capability, swallow still owns and manages the Zone locally.
- Allowed meaning: A managed record identified by an opaque, stable swallow-issued `zoneId`, carrying a `name` that is unique within its Site, an optional `description`, and the `siteId` it belongs to. A Server may be assigned to at most one Zone at a time; the assignment is written through to the provisioner and its observed effect appears on the Server's provider-observed zone. Realization in the provisioner is best-effort by capability: a Site whose provisioner cannot express zones is a Zone that exists only in swallow.
- Disallowed meaning: Not a Site (a Zone lives inside one Site; a Site is a location, a Zone is a grouping inside it), not a Pool (a Pool partitions resources for allocation; a Zone groups for availability/fault/organization), not a Kubernetes/Slurm Platform, not a physical-placement hierarchy level (datacenter/room/rack), and not the provisioner's opaque zone label carried through untouched — that earlier opaque pass-through is superseded by this swallow-owned model.
- Synonyms: None. (A MAAS "physical zone" is the provider realization of a Zone, not a separate term.)
- Deprecated terms: None.
- Examples: "Create a Zone `rack-a` in the lab Site; MAAS gains a matching physical zone because its provisioner is grouping-capable." / "Assign `gpu-node-01` to Zone `rack-a`; the provisioner records the machine's zone and reconcile mirrors it onto the Server." / "A Site backed by a provisioner without zones still lists its swallow Zones."
- Related terms: Pool, Site, Server, Integration, OS Provisioning Provider, Machine.
- Change note: Added 2026-09-14 to make Zone a swallow-owned, Site-scoped managed concept surfaced under the dashboard *Infrastructure* area, with optional provider realization. This supersedes the note on the `provisioning` `Machine` that a provisioner's zone label is "carried through opaquely and not mapped onto any swallow hierarchy". See decision 029.
